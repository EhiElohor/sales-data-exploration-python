# Sales Data Exploration (Python)

Exploring a US office supplies sales dataset (9,800 rows) from the Techcrush Data Science class.

## What I did
- Loaded and inspected the data
- Cleaned it: day-first dates, missing postal codes, data types, unused columns
- Answered business questions on categories, regions, growth and shipping
- Visualised results and wrote findings for a store manager

## Key findings
- Sales grew 50.5% from 2015 to 2018
- The West sells about 1.8x more than the South
- Technology is the top category at 36.6% of sales

## Tools
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn

The dataset is from the class and is not included in this repo.
